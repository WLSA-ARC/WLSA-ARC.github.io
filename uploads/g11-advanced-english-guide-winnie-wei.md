# G11 Advanced English Guide Notes

## OPTIC

OPTIC（similar with PEEL）。每个 part 一定要四句话以上。

### O = Overview

1. Overall composition: symmetrical/asymmetrical, horizontal/vertical composition.
2. Overall tonality: cool tone, warm tone, high contrast, low saturation.
3. Work type: documentary photography, abstract painting（black and white?）.

可以写一点模糊的 first impression，不要给情绪性的词汇。

### P = Parts

1. **Line**: straight/curved, thick/thin, leading line, the path of viewer’s gaze.
2. **Shape + form**: geometric form, organic shape; flat / three-dimensional.
3. **Light + shadow**: direction of light source, highlights, shadows and their symbolism.
4. **Color**: vivid?（relate to the atmosphere then deeper to the theme）, vibrant, muted.
5. **People**: gesture, facial expression（not just simple emotion, but describe each sense organs）, clothing, symbolic meaning of objects, how do they face the camera（turning back? Relate to their emotion and conditions）.
6. **Background**: empty? Any symbol?

### T = Title

1. Literal meaning of the title.
2. Whether the title reshapes our first impression.
3. Relationship: echo / contradiction / irony.
4. Metaphor or allusion created by the title.
5. “When and where” sometimes point out the background（then to the theme）.

### I = Interrelationships

1. Priority + hierarchy: focal point vs background.
2. Contrast: comparison of size, lightness and color.
3. Visual balance: whether visual weight is evenly distributed.
4. Relationship among foreground, middle ground and background.
5. Tension or conflict created by visual elements.
6. Visual elements sometimes guide the viewers to focus on sth. special（e.g. main character? An important symbol?）. Maybe title can help in this part.
7. **Color + background + emotion = atmosphere.**

写 layout 很好用，因为 layout 本身就是一种把画面各个部分放在一起的串联，前景中景，或者画面中间和两边。

无论画面是满的、有景物的，还是空的，都可以分析一下。并不是空的就不能写了，留白本身就是一种特点，或许可以反应人物孤独的心情？

### C = Conclusion

1. Themes.
2. Feelings and reflection evoked in viewers（the special audience）; purpose.
3. Connect to broader themes: social atomization, social injustice, power, identity, human condition（relate to the background like the year? E.g. the Great Depression? Wars?）.
4. Critique or social commentary.
5. Deep meaning shaped by cultural + historical context.

## Tone

- Analytical, impartial / neutral, logical, persuasive, assertive.
- Sympathetic, compassionate.
- Ironic.

分析顺序：

1. Tone.
2. Evidence.
3. Purpose.
4. Effect.

Probably includes the **tone shift**.

Think about these questions: What’s the tone? How is it? Why is the tone?（Relate to the characters’ emotion and the plot.）

Then to the character’s personality? Experience?（In the article.）Then to the theme.

## PEEL

**Point → Evidence → Explanation → Link（Conclusion）**

- Point 是浅层，link 是深层。
- Point 是小范围群体（如文章中的几个主人公），theme 更加 globally（如全体女性）。
- Evidence 最好是概括文章内容加一小部分分析，explanation 不要加太多主旨。

PEEL 所分析的文章本身就是一种 artwork，和 OPTIC 本质都是从浅层的画面往深层的主旨推导。
